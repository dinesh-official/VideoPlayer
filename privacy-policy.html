Privacy Policy for Video Player

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who we are

This policy applies to the Video Player Android app. For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.

Videos and on-device app data

Video Player reads videos through Android's media library so it can display and play them. It does not upload, copy, or share your videos. Watch history, playback positions, playlists, search history, bookmarks, and settings are stored on your device. You can clear supported app data in Settings or remove local app data by uninstalling the app.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show clearly identified banner and native ads. Ads may appear in library screens and some player screens, including below video details, in a side panel when playback is paused, and after playback. Ads do not cover the video.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and share an IP address, device or account identifiers, ad and app interactions (such as ad taps, app launches, and video views), and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. Google states that data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policies for details about Google's data practices and retention.

Consent and ad privacy choices

Where required, Google's consent message is presented before applicable ad requests. You can review or change available ad privacy choices in Video Player under Settings > Advertisement preferences. The app does not require an account.

Data retention and security

App library and playback data remains on your device until you clear it in Settings or uninstall the app. Google handles data collected by its advertising services under Google's policies. Data collected by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit according to Google.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The current version will be available at this webpage and within the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact details on the Video Player Google Play listing. Read the Terms and Conditions.
